About Carl’s Squeaky Clean Windows

Hello, I’m Carl Downer, founder of Carl’s Squeaky Clean Windows.

I started the business in 2013, when my family and I decided to settle in France after many years travelling around the world. We feel incredibly fortunate to call the beautiful Chamonix valley home, and I’m proud to have built my business here.

With experience cleaning windows in both England and Australia before arriving in the Alps, I’ve spent many years perfecting my craft. My aim has always been simple: to provide the cleanest, clearest windows in the Chamonix valley and beyond!

A personal service, with an eye for detail

I provide professional window cleaning throughout the valley, from Vallorcine to Megève. Whether it’s a small studio apartment, a family home, a large luxury chalet or a commercial property, every job receives the same care and attention.

As I carry out all the work myself, you get a personal, reliable service from someone who takes genuine pride in what he does. I believe the difference is in the details, and I put just as much effort into the last window of the day as I do the first.

Window cleaning to suit your needs

Whether you need a one-off clean after building work, a seasonal spring clean or a regular weekly or monthly service, I can tailor the service to suit your property.

As a permanent resident in the valley, I offer a reliable, year-round service, weather permitting. From everyday homes to luxury rental chalets, my goal is to leave your windows sparkling and one less chore on your to-do list.

No more ladders, no more cold hands — just beautifully clean windows!

You can expect a professional service, attention to detail and fair, transparent pricing.
