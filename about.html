About

Hello. I'm Carl Downer, founder of Carl's Squeaky Clean Windows. My aim is simple: the squeakiest, cleanest windows in the whole Chamonix valley and beyond.

I set up the company in 2013, when my family and I decided to settle in France after many years of travelling the world. We feel very fortunate to live in such a beautiful valley.

I have extensive experience in the industry, having made windows "squeaky and clean" across England and Australia before arriving here.

I work across the Chamonix valley and beyond, from Vallorcine in the east to Megève in the west. All the work is done by me, so you get a first-class personal service, with a smile.

Achieving beautiful, gleaming windows is an art in itself. I take pride in my work and put as much effort into the last window of the day as the first.

Whether you need an after-construction clean, a deep spring clean or a regular weekly or monthly service, you can rely on me. No more ladders and no more cold hands for you. A professional service at a very fair price.
